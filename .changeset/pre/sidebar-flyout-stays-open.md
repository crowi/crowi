The sidebar panel opened from the header on narrower windows now stays open while you move between pages with it, instead of closing after a page change; it still closes with its close button, Escape, or a click outside. The page tree also keeps showing while the next page's part of the tree loads, rather than briefly collapsing to a loading placeholder, both in that panel and in the sidebar on wide windows.
