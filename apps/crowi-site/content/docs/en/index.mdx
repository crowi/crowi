Crowi is a Markdown-based wiki application — open source software for sharing what your team knows.

You write pages in Markdown, and collaborative editing, full-text search, comments and notifications keep your team's knowledge growing.

You host it on your own server with Docker, and storage, search and renderers are all swappable plugins.
