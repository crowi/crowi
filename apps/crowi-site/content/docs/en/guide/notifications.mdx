Crowi delivers **notifications** when something happens on a page you are
involved with. Events such as a comment being added, a like, or a mention are
the subjects of notifications.

## How notifications are generated

Three things create a notification.

| What happened | Who is notified |
| ------------- | --------------- |
| Someone commented on a page | Everyone involved with the page (its creator, earlier commenters, earlier editors) |
| Someone liked a page | The same people |
| Someone mentioned you with `@username` in a body | You |
- Notifications for the same page and the same action are merged into a
  single notification within the last week, and when several people perform
  the same action they are aggregated as "action users".

## Notification states

A notification is unread, read, or opened.

- The **unread badge** on the notification bell counts the unread ones.
- "Mark all as read" in the notification list marks the unread ones read and
  clears the badge.
- Opening an individual notification marks that one as opened.

## The notification bell

A **notification bell** is shown in the screen header.

- When there are unread notifications, the bell icon gets a badge with the
  unread count (100 or more is shown as `99+`).
- The unread count is **refreshed automatically every 30 seconds** while the
  tab is active.
- Clicking the bell opens a dropdown that lists the latest notifications. The
  notification list is loaded when the dropdown is opened.
- "Mark all as read" at the top of the dropdown marks unread notifications as
  read in bulk.
- Clicking an individual notification marks it opened and takes you to the
  page.

## The notification list page

All notifications can be viewed on the notification list page at
`/_notifications`. You can scroll back through more notifications than the
dropdown shows, and clicking a notification here also navigates to the target
page.
